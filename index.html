The kitchen

Small kitchen, big tandoor.

Delhi Bites is a takeaway kitchen with one job: turn out unpretentious, properly spiced North-Indian food, cooked to order. Tandoori breads pulled from the oven by hand. Marinades that sit overnight. Curries built from a base gravy that smells like the kitchen door at a wedding.

The menu changes around what's good. We keep the list short because we'd rather get a handful of dishes right than promise everything. Walk in, look up at the board, ask what's good tonight.

Charcoal · clay · 480°C

Lit at four. Hot by five.

The tandoor is the centre of the kitchen — a clay belly fired with charcoal until the walls glow. Breads are slapped to the inside and pulled out blistered; skewers go down through the top, smoking as they cook.

What's on the board

A short, honest list of what the kitchen makes most nights. The full menu lives in-store — prices, specials and what's just come off the grill change with the week. Ring the bell, ask the counter.

01

From the tandoor

Marinated chicken, lamb and paneer cooked on skewers in the clay oven. Naans, kulchas and rotis pulled hot to order.

02

Curries & gravies

House gravy built slow — onion, tomato, ginger, garam masala — finished into whatever the night calls for. Mild through to chilli-forward.

03

Old Delhi street bites

Small plates from the city's lanes — chaats, samosas, kebabs that travel. Built for sharing or eating one-handed on the walk home.

04

Rice, breads & sides

Basmati cooked plain or biryani-style with whole spice. Hand-rolled breads. Pickles, raita, salad — the supporting cast that holds the meal together.

05

Sweet & finishing

A small handful of after — gulab jamun warm in syrup, kheer when it's on, kulfi from the freezer. Chai by the cup if you're staying a minute.

06

Vegetarian, by default

Half the menu is meatless by tradition, not by trend — dals, paneer, chana, aloo. Tell us if you need dairy- or gluten-aware and we'll point you to what works.
